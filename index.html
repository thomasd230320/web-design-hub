01 / Services

Three things, done properly.

No upsells, no agency padding. Every project includes all three.

01

Custom web design

Hand-coded sites built around your business — no templates, no page builder, no theme. The site looks like you because nothing else looks like it.

02

Mobile-first build

Most of your enquiries arrive on a phone. I design there first — the booking flow, the gallery, the consultation request — and scale up to desktop after.

03

SEO foundations

Clean HTML, schema markup, local search signals, fast page loads. Baked into the code, not bolted on later by a plugin.

I don't do branding, logos, photography, or social media. Just websites. If you need the others, I can point you at people who do them well.

02 / Process

Two to three weeks, end to end.

Five short stages. You always know what's happening and what comes next.

  1. Day 0

    We talk. Thirty minutes. I ask about your business, your clients, what's worked before. You ask anything you want. No pitch.

  2. Day 1

    Fixed quote. Within a working day of our call. Itemised, no surprises.

  3. Days 2–6

    Design. You see a real, browseable draft. Two short rounds of revision usually does it.

  4. Days 7–14

    Build. Hand-coded HTML, CSS and JS. Booking systems wired in, analytics set, Core Web Vitals tuned.

  5. Day 15

    Launch. I walk you through everything; you push the button. The code is yours — host it with me or anywhere else.

03 / Pricing

Honest, in a range.

A fixed written quote follows our first call. These are the bands most projects land in.

£400 — £900
A one- to three-page site. Brochure pages, contact form, booking link. The starting point for most clinics and small studios.
£900 — £2,000
Five to eight pages. Booking system integration (Square, Fresha, Stripe). On-page SEO and analytics setup. Where most commissions land.
£2,000 — £5,000
Larger sites, e-commerce, custom integrations, content systems. Always quoted properly after a longer conversation.

Hosting is optional — roughly £15 – £40 per month depending on what you need. I don't lock you in: you own your code and can move it anywhere.
