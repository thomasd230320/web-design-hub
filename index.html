Add-ons

I don’t charge for add-ons. I think they are basic functions of any good website, so every site comes with a selection of them, chosen to suit it.

Cloudflare

A free layer that sits in front of your site. It helps pages load faster around the world and blocks a lot of harmful traffic.

Google Analytics

Shows how many people visit, where they come from and which pages they read, so you can see what is working.

Google Business Profile

Your free listing on Google Search and Maps, with opening hours, photos, reviews and directions. I help set it up and link it to your site.

Google Search Console

A free Google tool that shows whether your pages are found and what people searched for before they visited.

Secure connection (HTTPS)

The padlock in the browser. It keeps what visitors type private and stops the warnings that scare people away.

Contact form with spam protection

Enquiries go straight to your inbox, with checks that block spam.

Sitemap

A list of all your pages for search engines, so none are missed.

Structured data

Hidden labels that tell search engines and AI what your business is, where it is and what you offer.

Social sharing previews

The picture and title that appear when your page is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook or LinkedIn.

Cookie notice

A simple banner asking permission for tracking cookies, added where your site needs one.

Browser and phone icons

The small icon in the browser tab and on a phone’s home screen.
