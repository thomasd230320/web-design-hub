FAQs

Q1

How long does it actually take to build my website?

Starter packages are typically live within 5 working days, Pro in 7–10 days, and Premium in 10–14 days. The timer starts the moment you sign off the brief and provide your content — we'll never leave you guessing what's next.

Q2

Do I need any technical knowledge to work with you?

None. We handle everything — domain, hosting, design, copy, the lot. You tell us about your business and provide any photos you'd like to use. If you don't have photos, we'll source professional stock imagery at no extra cost.

Q3

What's included in the hosting plans?

Hosting is tiered to match your site. £55/month for Starter (Essentials), £95/month for Pro (Standard), £145/month for Premium (Pro+), and £245/month for E-commerce (Commerce). Every plan covers secure hosting, automatic backups, security updates, content tweaks, and priority email and phone support. Higher plans include faster response times, more frequent content updates, and shop or booking-system maintenance.

Q4

Is monthly hosting required?

For Starter, Pro and Premium it's optional — you can pay once for the build and host it yourself or anywhere else. For the E-commerce tier ongoing hosting is required: a working online shop needs active care for payments, stock and security, and self-hosting genuinely risks breakage.

Q5

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The default is 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. For Premium projects we're happy to discuss a three-stage payment plan — just mention it when you get in touch.

Q6

What if I don't like the first design?

You'll get unlimited revisions within the scope of your package. We'd rather get it right than rush it out. If after honest feedback we still can't find a direction you love, you walk away with a full refund — no scripts, no chasing.
