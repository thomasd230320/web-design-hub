The process

From consultation to launch.

Four steps, each one deliberate. You always know what's happening, when, and what comes next — long before any code is written.

Stage i.

Consultation

A thirty-minute conversation about your brand, your treatments and the clientele you want to win. No jargon, no pressure. You receive a fixed, written proposal within a working day.

Complimentary · 30 minutes
Stage ii.

Strategy & wireframe

Before a pixel is drawn we agree on the architecture — what each page does, the customer journey, the booking flow. The shape is settled before the surface.

Days 1–3 · approval-led
Stage iii.

Design & refinement

Editorial layouts, refined together over a few short rounds. You see real, browseable drafts — not flat PDFs — and we polish until it reads exactly the way your treatments feel.

Days 4–9 · unlimited rounds in scope
Stage iv.

Build & launch

Hand-coded, fast and mobile-first. We connect your booking system, set the analytics, walk you through everything — and you press the launch button yourself.

Days 10–14 · you own everything
