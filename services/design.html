Why bespoke

People decide in three seconds.

Search engines can put you in front of people. They can't make those people click — and they certainly can't make those clicks turn into enquiries. That last bit is design's job.

A site that looks like every other site in your industry signals "average". A site built from a template signals "I didn't really care." A bespoke, hand-coded site signals something different — and that signal is worth more than every SEO trick combined.

  • Custom layouts, never templatesEvery page drawn for you. The grid, the spacing, the rhythm — built around your work, not around someone else's pre-baked shape.
  • Considered typographyFonts chosen for tone, paired for hierarchy, sized for readability on a phone. The single biggest visual decision a website makes.
  • Brand-true colour & detailIf you have a brand, I work within it. If you don't, I help you find one. Either way, every micro-decision points the same direction.
  • Professional polish, not pixel-perfectionismThe right details signal care without slowing the project down. I know which ones matter.

The cues that earn a click.

Three things visitors register, often without realising, in the first few seconds on your site. Get these right and the rest of the work has a chance.

01

Visual hierarchy

What's important looks important. What's secondary looks secondary. Eye knows where to land first — and never has to hunt.

02

Honest copy

Plain words. Specific claims. Nothing puffed. The fastest way to look like a real business is to write like one.

03

Quiet polish

Consistent spacing, line-lengths that read, no jagged edges. The kind of detail people notice only when it's missing.

How it works

Hand-drawn, hand-coded.

01 — Brief

A call. We talk about your customers, your competitors, and what "good" looks like to you. Then I write it up so we both know what we're building.

02 — Draft

Within 3–5 days you see a real browseable design, not a flat mockup. You click around, give honest feedback, and we shape it together.

03 — Build

Hand-coded HTML and CSS. No theme, no framework. The kind of code you could read on a Sunday and understand.

04 — Launch

Domain, hosting, indexing, Search Console. Then a handover doc, training video and one revision round in the first month — included.
